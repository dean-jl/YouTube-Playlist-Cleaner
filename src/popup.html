YouTube Playlist Cleaner

:

This extension can only be used on a YouTube playlist page.

Please navigate to a valid playlist and try again.

Help & Guide

Filter Options

  • Duplicate Videos: Detects videos appearing more than once in the playlist. The earliest/first instance is preserved, while subsequent copies are selected for removal.
  • Duration / Shorts: Target YouTube Shorts (≤ 60s) or filter videos shorter or longer than a custom minute and second threshold.
  • Watched Videos: Filter videos you have already watched (either any played progress or at least a specified percentage 1–100%).
  • Unavailable Videos: Targets private or deleted videos. (Requires selecting "Show unavailable videos" in YouTube's playlist menu first).
  • Age: Targets videos published older than a specified number of days, weeks, months, or years.
  • Title & Channel: Comma-separated keywords or exact phrases in double quotes (e.g. "let's play", tutorial).
  • Match Logic: Match ANY (OR) selects a video if it meets at least one active filter. Match ALL (AND) requires a video to satisfy every active filter simultaneously.
  • Dry Run: Scans and identifies matching videos, generating a full summary report without deleting anything.

Tools & Features

  • Export Playlist (CSV): Scans the current playlist and downloads a structured UTF-8 CSV spreadsheet with video titles, IDs, URLs, channel names, durations, and watched status. Great for backing up before mass cleaning!
  • Preferences Memory: Your filter choices and values are automatically saved and restored whenever you reopen the extension popup.

Quick Tips

  • Review the match count in the confirmation dialog before confirming deletion.
  • Do not interact with the browser tab or navigate away while the cleaning process is running.
  • If you need to stop the operation, click Cancel Operation in-page and allow it to finish cleaning up; do not immediately close the tab.

Troubleshooting

If the extension cannot connect or appears to stall, try these steps in order:

  1. Reload the playlist page and reopen the extension popup.
  2. Try a Dry Run first to confirm matches without making changes.
  3. If problems persist, open an issue on the extension store listing or repository with as much detail as possible.
